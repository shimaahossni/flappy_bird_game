### Color Scheme (Cyberpunk Theme)
- Primary: Deep Purple (#4A0080)
- Secondary: Electric Blue (#0080FF)

- Text: White (#FFFFFF)

### Character (Butterfly, not Bird)
- Design a colorful butterfly sprite
- Use multiple colors: Purple, Blue, Cyan
- Multiple animation frames for wing flapping
- Size: ~50x40 pixels (same as original)
